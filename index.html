PathShot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

No data collection

PathShot does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information processed by the app (GPS tracks, photos, camera settings) stays on your device and is never sent to any server.

Location

GPS data is recorded locally as GPX files stored on your device. It is never uploaded or shared with third parties.

Photos

PathShot reads and writes GPS coordinates to photos in your local Photos library. No photos or metadata are transmitted outside your device.

HealthKit

PathShot uses a HealthKit workout session solely to keep GPS running in the background on Apple Watch and iPhone. No health data is read or stored by the app.

Third-party services

PathShot uses no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
